02 / The artist

Andrei
Constantin.

Painter. Graphic artist.
Art restorer.

Trained in painting and graphic arts at Bucharest Art School, Andrei Constantin works with oil on canvas and pastels, with a particular focus on landscapes and still life.

His résumé records personal exhibitions in Romania between 1990 and 2015, and group exhibitions in Italy, Germany, France and Russia between 2003 and 2014, including exhibitions held by the Romanian Cultural Institute.

His work is held in private collections in Germany, Italy, the United States, the United Kingdom, Israel, France, Canada and New Zealand.
